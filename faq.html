Q1.Do I need to know swimming to participate in water sports?
> Not necessarily! Many water sports like jet skiing or banana boat rides do not require swimming skills. However, for activities like scuba diving, basic swimming is helpful.

Q2.Is safety gear provided?
> Yes, all necessary safety equipment such as life jackets, helmets, and diving gear are provided by the activity organizers.

Q3.What is the minimum age requirement?
> It depends on the activity. For example:
- Jet Skiing: 16+
- Scuba Diving: 12+
- Parasailing: 14+
(Always check the specific activity guidelines.)

Q4.How can I book an activity?
> Simply go to the “Register Now” section, choose your sport, location, and date, then submit the form!

Q5.Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?
> Yes, you can reschedule up to 24 hours before the activity time. Cancellation policies may vary based on the provider.

Q6.What should I wear for water sports?
> Wear comfortable swimwear or quick-dry clothing. Avoid heavy shoes or accessories. Don’t forget sunscreen!

Q7.Are water sports safe for first-timers?
> Absolutely! All activities are guided by certified professionals. First-timers are briefed and monitored throughout.

Q8.Can I bring my phone or camera during the activity?
> We recommend waterproof gear only. Some locations offer GoPro footage or rental options for underwater photos.

Q9.What if the weather is bad?
> In case of heavy rain, strong winds, or unsafe conditions, the activity may be postponed or refunded based on the situation.

Q10.Do I need a medical certificate?
> For basic activities, no. But if you have health conditions (like asthma or heart problems), please consult a doctor or inform the operator beforehand.
