Top Kayaking Locations in India

Alleppey, Kerala

Why: Backwater kayaking with lush surroundings

Best For: Nature lovers, beginners

Rishikesh, Uttarakhand

Why: Ganges rapids and mountain views

Best For: Thrill seekers, experienced kayakers

Goa

Spots: Mandovi River, Sal Backwaters

Best For: Easy kayaking, sightseeing

Andaman Islands

Spots: Havelock, Baratang

Why: Kayak through mangrove forests and lagoons

Zanskar River, Ladakh

Why: High-altitude kayaking with glaciers

Best For: Advanced adventurers

Kumarakom, Kerala

Why: Calm waters on Vembanad Lake

Best For: Scenic and relaxing paddling

Tehri Lake, Uttarakhand

Why: Mountain views and calm lake kayaking

Sitamata Jungle, MP

Why: Jungle kayaking with rich wildlife

Dandeli, Karnataka

Why: Forest kayaking on the Kali River

Chaliyar River, Kerala

Why: Eco-tourism and peaceful river paddling
